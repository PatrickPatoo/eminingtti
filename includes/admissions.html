Admission Requirements

We offer various programs depending on your KCSE results. Below are the general requirements:

📜

Artisan Courses (2 Years)

For students who sat for KCSE. No minimum grade required. We believe everyone deserves a chance to learn a skill.

🎓

Craft Certificate (2 Years)

Requires a minimum KCSE grade of D or D- (or equivalent). Leads to Diploma courses.

📈

Diploma Courses (3 Years)

Requires a minimum KCSE grade of C- (or a relevant Craft Certificate). Advanced hands-on training.

How to Apply

You can apply online via the Student Portal, or visit the institution in person for manual application. Bring the following documents:

  • Copy of KCSE Result Slip
  • Copy of National ID or Birth Certificate
  • Two passport-size photos
